## Findings

### F1. Focused table sort headers are hidden under the sticky checkbox and device columns  [S3]
- Where: Browse table `#tbl`, header buttons `#th-*`, 760 px and up (measured at 800 and 1280). Theme-independent.
- Steps (keyboard only): 1. Clear storage, set the viewport to 800×800 (or 1280×800), open `#browse` (table layout, `#tblw.scrollLeft` 0). 2. Focus `#lay-cards`. 3. Press Tab 12 times to move across all header buttons; the browser scrolls `#tblw` to the right by itself. 4. Press Shift+Tab 11 times to move back. 5. After each key, read the rect of the focused element and `elementFromPoint` at its centre.
- Steps (mouse scroll first): 1. As above, then put the mouse over the table and scroll the wheel horizontally to the right end (`scrollLeft` 825 at 800). 2. Focus `#lay-cards` and press Tab once.
- Expected: The focused control is visible. The browser scrolls it into the part of `#tblw` that the sticky columns do not cover (WCAG 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured).
- Actual, keyboard only: forward Tab hides `#th-soc` at 800 (0 px visible at `scrollLeft` 286). Shift+Tab back leaves 8 of 11 buttons fully hidden at 800 (`th-ram`, `th-hz`, `th-res`, `th-screen` at 825; `th-cpu`, `th-soc` at 525; `th-weight`, `th-price` at 166), and 3 at 1280 (`th-soc`, `th-dims`, `th-weight` at 345). No pointer is needed.
- Actual, mouse scroll first: the Tab focuses `#th-price` at x 504..543. The sticky device column ends at x 544, so 0 of 39 px are visible, and the centre hit is `th.stk.c1`. Only a 2 px sliver of the focus ring shows. Forward Tab also lands fully hidden on `#th-ram` (0/32 px) and almost hidden on `#th-cpu` (9/59 px). Shift+Tab back across the header: 8 of 11 buttons are fully hidden at 800 px (`th-ram`, `th-hz`, `th-res`, `th-screen`, `th-g3d`, `th-soc`, `th-weight`, `th-price`), and 3 at 1280 px (`th-soc` 450..480, `th-dims` 318..372, `th-weight` 253..299, sticky edge 544). The browser treats these buttons as already in view, because they sit inside the scrollport, so it does not scroll them out from under the sticky cells. Enter then sorts by a column that the user cannot see. Row controls are not affected: `#q-`, `#open-` and `#ref-` sit in the sticky cells and stay visible at `scrollLeft` 345 and 400.
- Evidence: `after Tab from layout segs: th-price [504..543] stickyEnd 544 sl 36 visibleW 0/39 centerHit sticky stk c1`; 1280: `th-soc [450..480] stickyEnd 544 sl 345 visibleW 0/30 centerHit sticky stk c1`. Screenshot `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-n0k0m3-Programming-handheld-atlas/c2568e76-13ba-4d96-a37b-14b7fe0fee0b/scratchpad/qa/regress-responsive/sticky-focus-800.png`. Script `tab2.js`, output `tab2.txt`, in the same folder.
- Code: site/src/index.html:193 `.tblw { overflow-x: auto; }` has no `scroll-padding-inline-start`. The sticky columns at lines 195 to 198 (`.stk.c0` left 0, `.stk.c1` left 36 px) cover 271 px of the scrollport with the default columns. A candidate fix (not tested) is `scroll-padding-left` on `.tblw` equal to the sticky width, set in CSS or measured after `renderHead`. Also, `.thb { margin: -2px -4px }` (line 204) puts the left 4 px of `#th-price` under the device column even at `scrollLeft` 0 (x 540 vs 544).
- Reproduced twice from clean state: yes (two clean keyboard-only runs and two clean wheel-first runs at each of 800 and 1280, identical values; scripts `kbonly.js` and `tab2.js`)

### F2. Dark: the brand fill of a checked box and an on switch is under 3:1 against its surroundings  [S4]
- Where: Browse `.qbox:checked`, `#f-usb4` and `#tf-usb4` on, Compare `#hide-same` (on by default). Dark theme only.
- Steps: 1. `set-color-scheme dark`, clear storage, seed a queue of 2, reload at 1280. 2. Compute the WCAG ratio of the `.qbox:checked` background against the row background, and of the on-state `.sw` track against the panel. 3. Click `#f-usb4` and compute the track pair again.
- Expected: UI marks reach 3:1 against adjacent colours (WCAG 1.4.11).
- Actual: The fix for 27 moved these fills from `--accent` (`#499fff`) to `--bg-brand` (`#1f6fc0`). In dark the fill is 2.92:1 against `--bg` `#222731` and 2.53:1 against the reference row `#1a3250`. The old build gave 5.48:1 and 4.76:1. The on track against the off track is 1.72:1 in dark (3.85:1 in light). Light is fine (5.4:1 and 4.77:1).
- Why S4 and not S3: the state cues that matter still pass. The white tick and the white knob reach 5.13:1 on the fill and about 15:1 against the panel, and the knob position changes. The near-miss is the fill outline only.
- Evidence: `qboxCheckedFillPlainRow #1f6fc0 vs #222731 = 2.92`, `qboxCheckedFillRefRow #1f6fc0 vs #1a3250 = 2.53`, `usb4 pressed true track #1f6fc0 vs #222731 = 2.92, knob vs track = 5.13, knob vs panel = 14.97` (script `uimarks.js`).
- Code: site/src/index.html:168 `[aria-pressed="true"] > .sw { background: var(--bg-brand) }`, line 228 `.qbox:checked { background: var(--bg-brand); border-color: var(--bg-brand) }`. One option: keep `--bg-brand` for the fill and add a 1 px `--accent` border or ring in dark.
- Reproduced twice from clean state: yes (contrast run at 1280 and 400, and the `uimarks.js` run; static CSS)

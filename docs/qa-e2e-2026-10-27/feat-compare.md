## Observations (not bugs)
- 3D coherence (asked for): the 3D view extrudes the closed footprint, and the 2D front view draws the device open. Both are true to the data, but they disagree. Without a 3D-specific note, a user sees a 23 mm closed slab in one tab and a 320 mm tall open device in the other. F1 covers the screen on the slab.
- The dashed `line.hinge` sits on the same y as the solid bottom stroke of the lid and the solid top stroke of the deck. In light mode it reads as a thicker joint line, not as a dashed hinge. The two halves stay clear because of the rounded corners (`hinge-sheet-1280.png`, `cmp-side-wm2.png`).
- The card silhouettes are not to one scale, and they were not before. Non-clamshells draw at 0.49 to 0.63 px/mm. Clamshells now draw at 0.27 to 0.41 px/mm, because the 96 px height cap (line 1418, `cardHtml`) applies to the doubled height. For example, a Win Max 2 deck card is 68 px wide and a Steam Deck card is about 177 px wide, while the real widths are 227 mm and 298 mm.
- `lidScreen` (line 763) clamps the width and the height of the screen on their own to 98 %. A screen that overflows the lid in one axis would lose its aspect ratio. No device reaches this code today, because all 28 clamshells have a precomputed screen in the outline data.
- `suiplay-0x1`: the bottom-left corner of the data screen rect (`outlines.top.screen`) lies 0.06 mm below the traced outline edge (99.86 vs 99.80). This is below one pixel at any size, it comes from the data, and the clamshell change did not cause it.
- The adds from `#sq-dd` keep focus in `#sq` (line 1970, click handler `case "add"`), not on `#cref2-<id>`, while a slot remains. This lets a user add more devices in a row. The `cref2-` fallback runs only when the slot goes away.
- `ArrowDown` in `#sq` does not move into the dropdown. Keyboard users reach the items with Tab. This matches the code (no arrow-key handler).

          Notes

          PassMark scores

          A PassMark score is the average of many user uploads. The uploads mix power limits and host devices. A handheld at 15 W can score below its listing. Use the scores as a rough guide only.

          Stated and measured weight

          The cards and the table show the maker's stated weight. When a reviewer weighed a unit, the device sheet also shows that measured weight and its source. A row with no stated weight uses the measured weight.

          As held

          Every size and weight describes the device as you hold it to play. The controllers are attached. The battery pack is on. The lid of a clamshell is open. The overlay and the cards draw the same configuration.

          Derived values

          dmax is the thickest point. For a clip-on battery pack, it is the body thickness plus the pack thickness. The GPD Win 5 is 24.2 mm plus 18.1 mm, so dmax is 42.3 mm. When a pack is on, its weight is added to the body weight.

          Capacitive sticks

          GPD and OneXPlayer call some sticks capacitive. Some reviews call the same sticks Hall effect. The atlas keeps the maker's word. The device sheet lists the other claim under Conflicts. TMR (tunnel magnetoresistance) is a separate class from Hall.

          Unshipped rows

          A card that says Pre-order or Announced is for a device that has not shipped. Its specs can change before release.

          Outlines

          Top views come from official renders where possible. A traced outline is fitted to the stated width and height. A spec box is a rounded rectangle from the stated sizes.

          Makers rarely publish a clean side render. Every side view is therefore a spec box from the stated height and thickness. The dashed rectangle marks dmax.

          Data and sources

          The idea comes from retrocatalog.com. This atlas adds the size overlay, separates TMR from Hall sticks, and adds port and SSD detail.
